# Thirty-paper corpus rationale

M006 expands the completed M005 10-paper import-model baseline to 30 papers to discover deviations and recurring patterns. The selection keeps all 10 M005 papers for direct comparison and adds 20 deterministic local candidates selected from available research/cache evidence.

This is a diagnostic corpus only: it does not authorize production KG import, embeddings, vector generation, or LadybugDB writes.

## Selection policy

1. Include all M005 gold-corpus papers as baseline overlap.
2. Add expansion papers deterministically by local availability score and paper id.
3. Record only paths/status/risk tags, not raw paper text or chunk text.

## Counts

- Total papers: 30
- M005 overlap: 10
- Expansion papers: 20
