## Exporter geometry/text-quality follow-up (2026-10-13)

Executed the existing extractor's `classify_page_lines` (read-only, no export) through project native `/usr/bin/python3` + PyMuPDF1.28.0 over11 pinned pages/5PDFs; diagnostic in `geometry-audit.json`,script in `geometry-audit.py`,input hash manifest in `geometry-samples.json`. This is a bounded specimen audit, not a corpus estimate or oracle.

The cut is based on **line start**: `x0>0.65*W`, not an entire right-column region. Ordinary two-column lines beginning near0.5W remain. On ACM specimen pages1–2,11056of11134raw nonblank line characters stay in body (~99.3%);72move to margin and6header/footer. Therefore “drops the arXiv right column” is too broad a claim. Real failure is reading-order mixing: sorting retained lines by(y,x) interleaves columns. ACMpage2 places left sentence ending `mes-` beside right sentence `This resulted in a huge backlog...`; regex dehyphenation then can concatenate different-column words. High body-char retention is not valid reading order or faithful scientific extraction.

Rightmost author names/emails move to margin_notes on paper specimens; article evidence counts only section.text and omits margin_notes. On Tufte page50,3084raw chars yield2382body,451margin,64discard and187header/footer. Fixed50pt/70pt vertical cut and65%-width/numeric thresholds are specimen-specific, not measured universal layout. Numeric rule also drops legitimate short tokens; sample1203.2295page1 drops `The` solely because rightmost short line. Do not edit these frozen thresholds during the corpus run.

TOC stage marks furniture but does not remove its section.text from old section/body score. Same-page bookmarks and absent heading anchors can produce overlapping/repeated text slices; volume is not unique conserved content. Field/conservation/read-order diagnostics must stay separate from speed/volume and from the table's pair-label accuracy.

Raw pdftotext is an independent unfiltered PDF extraction reference, **not publisher groundtruth or scientific gold**. It can disagree on reading order,formulas,ligatures and hyphenation. A metric based on it should be labelled PDF text retention/coverage diagnostic. Publisher HTML/gold-F1 requires separate suitability,work/revision and source hash binding; no automatic graph/import implication follows.
