Versioning and Updates.

 As new papers are added to the corpus, we incrementally update the knowledge graph by detecting new entities, merging duplicates, and updating citation counts. Entity linking ensures that mentions in new papers are connected to existing canonical entities, maintaining graph coherence over time.
